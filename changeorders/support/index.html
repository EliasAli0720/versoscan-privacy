Addtape Support

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Contact us

Email hello@finecore.dev. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and the Addtape version (shown in the App Store under Version History).

Try it with the sample job

On first launch, tap Try a sample job. You can also open it later from Settings, under About. The sample job is for practice: it doesn't count as your free job, and its PDFs are marked SAMPLE.

Your first job is free

Your first job is free, from the first extra to the final invoice. Starting another job needs Addtape Pro, a one-time in-app purchase. There is no subscription. Jobs you already have stay open, and you can always print, send, export and back them up.

Restore a purchase

Open Addtape, go to Settings, then About, and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Addtape Pro is tied to that Apple Account, so it carries over to a new iPhone or a reinstall.

Back up and move to a new iPhone

With iCloud Drive turned on, Addtape saves a backup to its own folder in your iCloud Drive. To save one right away, go to Settings, then Backup, and tap Back up now. On a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account, Addtape offers to restore your records when you first open it.

You can also go to Settings and tap Export full backup to save a backup file anywhere you choose, and Import a backup to bring it back. Deleting the app without iCloud or an exported backup deletes your records.

Camera access

Addtape asks for the camera the first time you add an extra. If you turned access off, you can still pick a photo from your library, or turn the camera back on in the Settings app under Addtape.

Not legal advice

Addtape is a record-keeping tool. It doesn't give legal advice and doesn't process payments. Rules for change orders differ from state to state; check the rules that apply to your work.

Privacy

No account, no analytics, and your data is never sent to us. Read the full Addtape privacy policy.
