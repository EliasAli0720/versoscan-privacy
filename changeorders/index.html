Addtape Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Addtape (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a change-order app for contractors on iPhone, operated by FineCore. It is built so that we never receive your data: there is no account, no server, and no analytics, advertising or other third-party SDKs.

Information we do not collect

  • No account or sign-in is required to use the app.
  • We do not operate a server. Your jobs, extras, change orders, signatures, photos, payments and invoices are never sent to us or to any third party.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.

How your data is handled

  • Storage: your records stay on your iPhone. If iCloud Drive is turned on for Addtape, the app also saves a backup file to its own folder in your iCloud Drive (Apple's service, under your Apple Account), so you can restore your records on a new iPhone. We cannot access that folder.
  • Your clients' details: names, addresses, signatures and amounts you enter for your clients are kept in the same places as the rest of your records, and nowhere else.
  • Camera and photos: the camera is used only when you add a photo to an extra. Photos you take or pick are stored with their location and other metadata removed.
  • Copies, PDFs, CSV files and backups: when you print, send or export, the file goes only where you choose, for example a printer, Messages, Mail or the Files app.

Purchases

Addtape Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. We do not operate a payment server and do not receive or store your payment details. Purchase status is checked on your device through Apple's StoreKit.

Addtape does not process payments between you and your clients. Payments you log in the app are notes kept with your records.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, because we do not collect it in the first place.

Deleting your data

  • On your iPhone: deleting the app deletes the records stored on your iPhone. Signed change orders are locked in the app and can't be edited or deleted one by one. Before you delete the app, export a backup of any records you need to keep.
  • In iCloud: to delete the backup, open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Storage (or Manage Account Storage), choose Addtape and delete its data.
  • Files you exported or sent: these are wherever you saved or sent them. Delete them there.

Children's privacy

The app is made for contractors and is not directed at children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, for material changes, note them in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@finecore.dev. For help with the app, see Addtape Support.
