Insuplate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Insuplate (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a carb counter and logbook for adults with type 1 diabetes, for iPhone and Apple Watch, operated by FineCore. There is no account and no sign-in. The app has no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or attribution SDKs, and we do not track you.

Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy, including your rights under Washington law, is part of this page.

The short version

  • Only your meal leaves your phone: a meal or menu photo, or the words you say or type about a meal, with the technical details listed below. It goes to Google Gemini to estimate carbs, and only after you tap Allow.
  • Your glucose, your insulin units, your log and your Apple Health data stay on your phone. They are never sent to Google Gemini or to anyone else.
  • The app does not look up restaurant or chain menus in any outside database. It sends dish names nowhere.
  • You can stop sending at any time in Settings.

What is sent, and when

Before anything is sent, the app shows a sheet that says: “Your meal photo, voice or text goes to Google Gemini to estimate carbs.” Nothing is sent until you tap Allow. If you tap Not now, nothing is sent and the app keeps working with typed carbs, saved meals, label reading and your log. The same sheet appears on every path that can send a meal: the app, the Scan control on the Lock Screen, Siri and Apple Watch.

After you allow it, each estimate sends only:

  • A meal or menu photo you take for a scan, resized to at most 1024 pixels on its long side, with location and other photo metadata (EXIF and GPS) removed; or
  • The words of your meal, typed or spoken. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone or Apple Watch; the sound itself never leaves your devices; or
  • A fix: when you correct an estimate (“only 2 slices”), the list of items on that estimate and your words;
  • plus fixed instructions from the app that ask for a carb estimate.

Each request also carries the technical details any app request to this service needs: the app's bundle ID and version, its Firebase app ID, a Firebase API key that only works for this app, and a short-lived App Check token, made with Apple's App Attest, that shows the request came from a genuine copy of the app. On first use, Apple's App Attest makes a key for this copy of the app. Google keeps it to check that later requests come from a genuine copy. It isn't linked to your name, email or Apple Account. No name, email, account or user ID is sent. As with any internet connection, the service sees the IP address the request comes from.

The purpose is to give you a carb estimate, nothing else.

Who receives it

Requests go through Google's Firebase AI Logic service to the Gemini model on Google Cloud's Agent Platform, processed in Google Cloud's United States multi-region. Under Google Cloud's terms:

  • Google does not use this data to train or fine-tune its AI models without our permission, and we have not given it.
  • Google may cache requests to answer faster. Cached data is deleted no later than 24 hours after it is written.
  • If Google's automated safety systems flag a request as possible misuse, Google may keep it for up to 90 days in the same US region, and authorized Google staff may review it.

See Google's pages on abuse monitoring and data caching, and the Google Cloud Service Specific Terms.

Google receives this data only under these terms, which protect it at least as well as this policy does.

We do not keep copies of your photos or words, and we have not turned on request logging for this service, so we cannot see what you send.

What stays on your phone

  • Your log: meals, carbs, insulin units, glucose you type, notes, saved meals and the photos of meals you log. It is stored in the app on your iPhone. We do not run a server and the app does not sync your log to iCloud. Your log is included in your iPhone backups, which Apple keeps under your Apple Account.
  • Apple Health: if you connect it, the app reads glucose and insulin, and checks which apps write carbs or insulin, to show what each meal did. It writes the carbs and insulin you log, unless you turn that off. Apple Health data is used only to show your own record in the app. It is never sent off your phone, never used for advertising, and never shared or sold.
  • On-device features: reading a nutrition label, searching foods (a built-in copy of the USDA food table), speech recognition, and matching a scan to your past meals all run on your iPhone and send nothing.
  • Scan counts and purchases: your remaining free scans are kept in your iPhone's Keychain. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details.
  • Apple Watch, widgets, Siri and the Lock Screen: they work with the app on your iPhone. A meal you say on Apple Watch is sent only through your iPhone, only after you allow it, exactly as described above.
  • Copy and export: the copy button puts only the carb number on your clipboard. A PDF or CSV export goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Your choices

  • Stop sending: in the app, open Settings and turn off Send meals for AI estimates. Nothing more is sent. Turning it back on shows the same sheet first. Typed carbs, saved meals, label reads and your log keep working.
  • What was already sent: nothing in a request names you and we keep no copy, so we cannot find or delete a single past request. Google deletes it on the schedule above.
  • Delete your data: delete entries in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPhone. Carbs and insulin the app wrote to Apple Health stay in Apple Health until you delete them there.
  • Apple Health access: change or remove it in the Health app (your profile, then Apps, then Insuplate).
  • Camera and microphone: turn them off in the iOS Settings app. Typing still works.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent your information, share it for advertising, or track you across apps or websites. The only third party that receives anything is Google, for the carb estimate described above.

Age

Insuplate is for adults 18 and over and is rated 18+ on the App Store. It is not directed at anyone under 18.

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

This section is our consumer health data privacy policy under Washington's My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373). It applies to everyone who uses Insuplate.

  • What we collect, and why: because Insuplate is for people with type 1 diabetes, what you send for an estimate could be linked to a health condition. That is the meal or menu photo, the words of your meal, or a fix, plus the technical details that come with each request (the IP address and the App Attest key described above). We collect it only to give you the carb estimate you asked for. Your log (meals, carbs, insulin units, glucose, notes, saved meals) and the Apple Health data the app reads are used only to show your own record on your phone. We never receive them.
  • Where it comes from: from you, when you take a photo or say or type a meal; and from Apple Health, only with your permission and only on your phone.
  • Sharing and selling: we do not share or sell consumer health data.
  • Third parties: Google receives the request as our processor, only to produce the estimate, under the terms described above. No one else receives it.
  • Your rights: you can ask us to confirm whether we collect your consumer health data, to give you a copy of it and the list of third parties that received it, and to delete it. You can withdraw your consent at any time.
    • To withdraw consent: in Settings, turn off Send meals for AI estimates.
    • To delete your log: delete entries in the app, or delete the app.
    • Anything else: email hello@finecore.dev with the subject “Consumer health data request”.
    We answer within 45 days, free of charge. Nothing in a request names you and we keep no copy, so in most cases our answer will be that we hold no consumer health data about you.
  • Appeal: if we decline your request, reply to our answer to appeal. We decide appeals within 45 days and tell you why. If we deny your appeal, you can complain to the Washington State Attorney General: www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above. If what the app sends changes, the app will ask again before sending anything new.
