Insuplate Support

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Estimates can be wrong. Check with your doctor before changing how you dose. Insuplate counts carbs and keeps your log. It never works out, suggests or rounds an insulin dose.

Contact us

Email hello@finecore.dev. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and the Insuplate version (shown in the App Store under Version History).

AI estimates

The first time you scan or say a meal, Insuplate asks before sending it for an estimate. Tap Allow to send it, or Not now to type your carbs instead. To stop sending later, open Settings in the app and turn off Send meals for AI estimates. Typed carbs, saved meals, label reads and your log keep working without it.

If you see “Estimates are paused for now”, the estimate service is unavailable. Type your carbs, use a saved meal or scan a label; your log still works. Estimates come back on their own, with no update needed.

Glucose and Apple Health

Insuplate reads glucose from Apple Health, or you can type it. Dexcom reaches Apple Health 3 hours late. Libre apps don't reach it without a third-party bridge. If no glucose shows after a meal, check that your sensor's app writes to Apple Health and that Insuplate can read glucose (Health app, your profile, then Apps, then Insuplate).

If an automated insulin delivery app uses Apple Health, Insuplate won't write carbs or insulin there unless you turn that on in Settings, and it leaves that app's insulin out of your log, last dose and exports.

Free scans and Plus

You start with 30 AI scans. When they run out, 10 more arrive at the start of each month, beginning the month after. Your log, memory, saved meals, typed carbs, label reads and exports are always free. Plus gives you more AI scans, as a yearly subscription or a one-time purchase.

Restore a purchase: open Settings in the app and tap Restore, using the same Apple Account you bought with. Cancel the yearly plan in your Apple Account settings: Settings app, your name, then Subscriptions.

Apple Watch

The Apple Watch app works with Insuplate on your iPhone, so keep your iPhone nearby. Turn the Digital Crown to log insulin, starting from 0, or say a meal. Your log stays on your iPhone.

Export for your clinic

Open Settings in the app and tap Export PDF or CSV. The file goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Privacy

No account and no analytics. Glucose, insulin units and Apple Health data stay on your phone. Read the full Insuplate privacy policy.
