VersoScan Support

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Contact us

Email hello@finecore.dev. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and the VersoScan version (shown in the App Store under Version History).

Please don't email us your documents. VersoScan never sends your documents to us, and we'd rather not receive them. A short description of the problem, or a screenshot with personal details covered, is enough.

Restore a purchase

Open VersoScan, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Your purchase is tied to that Apple Account, so it carries over to a new iPhone or a reinstall.

Cancel or manage a subscription

In VersoScan, tap Manage Subscription in Settings. Or, on your iPhone, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Apple handles all billing, so we can't see or change your payment details.

What's free

Scanning, text recognition, search, export, iCloud sync and the app lock are free, with no ads and no watermark. A number of translated pages each month are free too; the translate screen shows how many you have left before any translation starts. VersoScan Pro removes the monthly translation limit.

Translating without internet

Translation runs on your iPhone using Apple's Translation framework. The first time you translate between two languages, iOS may ask to download a language pack. After that, translation works offline.

About translation quality

Translations are machine-generated and meant to help you understand a document. They are not certified translations. For legal, medical, immigration or other official use, please use a certified human translator.

Privacy

No account, no analytics, and your documents are never sent to us. Read the full privacy policy.
