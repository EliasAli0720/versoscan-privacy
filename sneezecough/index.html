Sneeze Cough Recorder Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Sneeze Cough Recorder (“the app”, “we”, “us”) counts coughs and sneezes on iPhone and Apple Watch. It is published on the App Store by Elias Ali. It is built to need as little of your data as possible: there is no account, no server, and no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Information we do not collect

  • No account or sign-in is required to use the app.
  • We do not operate a server, and the app makes no network requests of its own. Your counts, sessions, sounds and settings are never sent to us or to any third party.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs of any kind.

How your data is handled

  • Microphone: the iPhone app uses the microphone only while a Listen session that you started is running. The session screen and the system microphone indicator show when it is listening; Stop ends it. Sound is analyzed on your iPhone with Apple's built-in sound classification, in memory, and then discarded. No audio is saved unless you turn on clips. The Apple Watch app does not use the microphone. Counting by hand never needs the microphone, and you can decline microphone access and keep using the rest of the app.
  • Clips (optional, off by default): if you turn on “Keep short clips”, the app keeps a clip of at most 3 seconds around each counted or possible cough or sneeze so you can check it. Clips stay on your iPhone only, are excluded from iCloud and device backups, are deleted automatically after 7 days, and are never included in reports or anything you share. Sounds where speech is detected are not kept. You can delete all clips at any time in Settings.
  • Storage: your counts, sessions and settings are stored on your iPhone and your Apple Watch. The two sync directly with each other; nothing goes through iCloud or any server we operate. If you back up your iPhone (iCloud Backup or a computer), the system backup includes the app's counts like other app data; clips are excluded.
  • Wrist-motion samples (Apple Watch, optional, off by default): if you turn on “Save wrist motion samples” in the Watch app and allow Motion & Fitness, your Apple Watch keeps short accelerometer samples (about 12 seconds each) around coughs and sneezes counted by hand or by listening, and some from quiet times, each labeled with how it was counted and reviewed. They stay on your Apple Watch only: they are excluded from backups and never sent to your iPhone, to us or to anyone else. Your iPhone shows only the status and how many are saved. At most 1,000 samples are kept, and each is deleted after 180 days. They are kept so a later version of the app may use them, on your Apple Watch, to help count coughs and sneezes. Nothing in this version uses them to count or detect anything. Recording pauses when the battery is low.
  • Apple Health (optional): only if you turn on “Save coughs to Health”, the app asks for permission to write to the Coughing symptom in Health. It writes only coughs you entered by hand or confirmed after a session, never possible ones. It does not read any Health data. Health has no sneeze type, so sneezes stay in the app.
  • Reports: PDF and CSV reports are created on your iPhone and leave it only through the share sheet you open.

Purchases

The one-time Pro purchase is handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit. We do not operate a payment server and do not receive or store your payment details. Purchase status is checked on your device through Apple's StoreKit APIs.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, because we do not collect it in the first place.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete all data (Settings) removes every count, session and clip from your iPhone, and from your Apple Watch the next time the two connect. Counts carried over from version 1.0 are no longer shown or used. The original version 1.0 records stay on your Apple Watch, unchanged, until a later update removes them; deleting the app from your Apple Watch removes them now. It also removes every wrist-motion sample from your Apple Watch.
  • Wrist-motion samples: delete them any time in the Watch app (Settings, Delete motion samples), or turn the setting off and choose Delete. You can also turn Motion & Fitness access off in the Watch's Settings.
  • You can turn off “Save coughs to Health” at any time. Coughs already saved stay in Health until you delete them in the Health app (Browse, Symptoms, Coughing).
  • You can turn the microphone or Health access off at any time in the iOS Settings app.
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children, consistent with the fact that it does not collect personal information from any user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, for material changes, note them in the app's release notes.
